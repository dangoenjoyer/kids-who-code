About Elijah

Hello young coders, my name is Elijah and it is my goal to make sure all kids can enjoy the amazing world of code. So that's why I made Kids Who Code, the first ever business to run coding lessons for kids by a kid! I always dreamed of being able to code when I was younger, but it was always too expensive or out of reach. Now it's not!

What you'll learn

Make things move

Bring sprites to life with motion, sound, and costumes.

Build real games

Create a game you can play, share with friends, and keep improving.

Think like a coder

Practice loops, variables, and fixing bugs, the skills behind every program.

How it works

  1. Pick a time and a platformLessons are after school, so choose a time that works for your family. They are online, on Zoom or Microsoft Teams, whichever you prefer.
  2. Code togetherWe build a project step by step, and you get to add your own ideas.
  3. Show it offFinish with a game you made yourself and can share.
